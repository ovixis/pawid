# Dog and cat vaccination: Italy and Spain (obligations and recommended schedules)

Research notes for the PawID timeline feature. Collected 2026-11-22. Every row has a source URL, an original-language quote with an English translation, and a `last_checked` date.

Ground rules used here:
- Only official or European sources: ministries, regional governments and their official bulletins, EU law and the Commission, Istituti Zooprofilattici, and European universities or boards. No WSAVA, AAHA or US sources.
- **VERIFIED** means I read the primary text myself on the date given. **SECONDARY** means the fact comes from an official summary or a press report, not from the legal text itself. **UNCLEAR** means the number could not be confirmed. Nothing has been filled in by guesswork.
- An "obligation" is a legal duty on the owner. A "recommendation" is advice from an official or European expert body.

## 0. EU baseline (applies in both countries, travel only)

Rabies vaccination is not required everywhere in the EU, but it is required to **move** a dog, cat or ferret between Member States. Since 22 April 2026 the legal basis is Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/131. It replaced Regulation (EU) No 576/2013, which applied to non-commercial movements up to 21 April 2026.

| Obligation (travel within the EU) | dog, cat, ferret | rabies | at least 12 weeks old when vaccinated; valid from 21 days after the primary protocol is completed; each booster within the validity of the previous dose (validity length set by the vaccine, not by the law) | "The animal was at least 12 weeks old at the date the vaccine was administered." / "The period of validity of the vaccination starts not less than 21 days from the completion" of the primary vaccination protocol. / "any subsequent vaccination was carried out within the period of validity of the preceding vaccination." | https://food.ec.europa.eu/animals/live-animal-movements/dogs-cats-and-ferrets/travelling-pet-within-eu_en | SECONDARY (Commission page; I could not download the EUR-Lex text of 2026/131: the PDF came back empty, see https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2026/131/oj) | 2026-11-22 |

App note: the "travel ready from" date is the date of the first rabies dose + 21 days. The earliest first dose is at 12 weeks, so a puppy can travel at 15 weeks at the earliest (12 + 3), unless the destination country allows the young-animal exception.

## 1. ITALY

### 1.1 Summary

- **No national legal obligation** to vaccinate pet dogs or cats against anything, rabies included, outside travel. Italy got its rabies-free status back in February 2013 (Ministry notes 3306 of 19/02/2013 and 3827 of 26/02/2013).
- Rabies vaccination becomes **compulsory locally** only through emergency orders:
  - 2009 to about 2013, north-east Italy (Friuli-Venezia Giulia, Veneto, Trento, Bolzano) after the fox rabies epidemic.
  - **2026, Comune di Vittorio Veneto (TV, Veneto)** after an imported rabies case in a dog (confirmed 28 May 2026). All dogs and cats in the municipality must be vaccinated. The order was reported to run for six months.
- I found **no recommended puppy or kitten schedule of its own** from the Ministero della Salute, FNOVI, ANMVI, SCIVAC or the IZS network. Italian veterinary academia translates and relays the WSAVA guidelines (e.g. University of Milan, Prof. P. Dall'Ara, WSAVA 2024 in Italian), which this project excludes. For cats there is a European body: the ABCD (European Advisory Board on Cat Diseases), whose authors include Italian academics (Marsilio, Pennisi); see section 3.

## 2. SPAIN

### 2.1 Summary

- Animal health is a regional matter, so rabies rules are set by each autonomous community (CA) and by Ceuta and Melilla.
- **MAPA/AEMPS note, June 2026 (VERIFIED):** "Actualmente la vacunación en perros es obligatoria en 15 Comunidades Autónomas (CCAA), en Ceuta y Melilla, con los siguientes planes de revacunación: - Anual: en 8 CCAA, Ceuta y Melilla -Bienal: en 1 CA -Según la frecuencia indicada en las especificaciones técnicas del fabricante: en 6 CCAA. La vacunación en gatos y hurones es voluntaria en la mayoría de las CCAA (en 11 de ellas para los hurones y en 12 para los gatos)". (EN: "Dog vaccination is currently mandatory in 15 CAs plus Ceuta and Melilla: annual in 8 CAs plus Ceuta and Melilla, every two years in 1 CA, per manufacturer specs in 6 CAs. Cat and ferret vaccination is voluntary in most CAs (11 for ferrets, 12 for cats).") URL: https://www.mapa.gob.es/es/dam/jcr:2ee5a018-22bd-4f40-b4c8-9bcbc2ea4d8c/Nota%20diferencia%20vacunaci%C3%B3n%20FT%20y%20normativa%20MAPA-AEMPS.pdf (AEMPS page dated 22/09/2026: https://www.aemps.gob.es/industria-farmaceutica/etiquetado-y-prospectos/frecuencias-de-revacunacion-antirrabica-para-perros-gatos-y-hurones/). last_checked 2026-11-22.
- My per-region mapping below **matches those counts exactly**:
  - Annual (8): Andalucía, Illes Balears, Canarias, Castilla-La Mancha, Castilla y León, Madrid, Murcia, Navarra.
  - Every two years (1): La Rioja.
  - Per vaccine specs (6): Aragón, Asturias, Cantabria ("anual o antes del final del periodo de validez"), Comunitat Valenciana (after a 1-year booster), Extremadura (since 2025), País Vasco.
  - Voluntary for dogs: **Cataluña** and **Galicia** (mandatory only for assistance dogs).
  
  Assigning Cantabria to "per specs" rather than "annual" is my **inference**: it is the only split that makes MAPA's numbers add up.
- The national baseline table is MAPA's *Plan de contingencia para el control de la rabia terrestre ... 2023*, Annex IV (status at 1 May 2023, approved by the Consejo Interterritorial in November 2023): https://www.riojasalud.es/files/content/salud-publica-consumo/epidemiologia/edo/inf-adicional/Plan%20contingencia%20Rabia_2023_12122023.pdf (VERIFIED, pp. 44-48). Two changes since then: Madrid went from voluntary to mandatory (Decreto 89/2025), and Extremadura went from annual to per-specs (Decreto 113/2025).
- **Ley 7/2023 (national animal welfare law)** sets no vaccine or schedule for owned pets. It refers to "vacunación obligatoria" defined elsewhere: for shelters handing over animals for adoption ("Cumplir con los requisitos mínimos veterinarios para la entrega de los animales correspondientes y los tratamientos mínimos estipulados en relación con la esterilización, la identificación, la desparasitación y la vacunación obligatorias"), for managed cat colonies ("Programa sanitario de la colonia ... incluyendo al menos la desparasitación, vacunación e identificación obligatoria"), and for imports and exports ("deberán cumplir los requisitos de identificación, edad, vacunación y tratamientos veterinarios obligatorios, establecidos en la correspondiente normativa de la Unión Europea y nacional y, en particular, la vacunación antirrábica"). VERIFIED in the BOE consolidated text: https://www.boe.es/buscar/pdf/2023/BOE-A-2023-7936-consolidado.pdf (2026-11-22).
- **Legal curiosity (UNCLEAR applicability):** the pre-constitutional national *Decreto de 17 de mayo de 1952* (BOE-A-1952-7152) is still in the BOE consolidated database "sin modificaciones". It says: "será obligatorio en todos los Municipios el registro y matrícula de los perros ... y la vacunación obligatoria de los mismos" and "Los perros que entonces tengan menos de seis meses de edad serán vacunados al cumplirlos". URL: https://www.boe.es/buscar/pdf/1952/BOE-A-1952-7152-consolidado.pdf. In practice MAPA treats the matter as regional, and Cataluña and Galicia treat dog vaccination as voluntary, so do not use this decree in the app. Flagged only for completeness.
- **Leishmaniosis (Andalucía):** the 2010 Order lists "Tratamiento contra Leishmaniosis" among mandatory treatments. The regional tax office (Consulta 14-2026, June 2026) treats the leishmaniosis **vaccine** as deductible and explicitly **not** the kennel cough vaccine: "la vacuna de la tos de las perreras ... no obligatorio conforme a lo dispuesto por la normativa actual". This also confirms the 2010 Order is current in 2026. URL: https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/sites/default/files/inline-files/2026/06/Consulta%2014-26%20web.pdf. My reading: the Order itself does not make the leishmaniosis vaccine compulsory; the tax office only says the vaccine cost counts as deductible.

App notes for Spain:
- The minimum age differs by region: "from 3 months", "12-16 weeks", "12-24 weeks", "12-28 weeks", or Murcia's "3,5 meses". Store a per-region rule with min_age and, where set, max_age_for_first_dose.
- Where boosters are "per specs", the due date comes from the validity the vet writes in the passport (Extremadura requires that date to be written down). The app should ask for "valid until" instead of computing it.
- Where the law says annual, show the legal annual date even if the product claims 3 years. MAPA says this explicitly: "la frecuencia de revacunación obligatoria estará supeditada a una evaluación de riesgo" ("the mandatory revaccination frequency depends on a risk assessment", not on the product's duration of immunity).
- Two-dose primary courses: Andalucía (2nd dose at +30 days), Castilla-La Mancha and Navarra (2nd within 1 month), Melilla (per MAPA).
